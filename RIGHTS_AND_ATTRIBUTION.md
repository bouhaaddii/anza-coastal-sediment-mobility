# Rights and attribution

This repository combines author-supplied survey points with third-party satellite, ocean, tide-model, bathymetry, and sediment-context products. The acknowledgements below do not replace the terms attached to each original product.

## Author-supplied Anza survey

The Anza bathymetric point files were provided by the study author and are included publicly at the author's request for study inspection and audit. Their vertical values remain relative to the local hydrographic zero of the Port of Agadir. No separate license for redistribution or reuse of these survey observations is assigned in this repository. Contact the study author before reuse beyond inspection and verification of this study.

## Copernicus Sentinel-2

The Sentinel-2-derived inputs are from Copernicus. Cite the exact product identifiers listed in `Data/Sentinel2/scene_catalog.csv` and follow the current Copernicus Data Space terms and attribution requirements: https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/terms-and-conditions

## Copernicus Marine Service

Ocean-colour, wave, and current products are from Copernicus Marine. Product IDs, dataset identifiers, versions, time spans, variables, and available DOIs are recorded in the source manifests under `Data/Copernicus_Marine/`. Cite the product DOI and acknowledge Copernicus Marine in accordance with its current guidance: https://help.marine.copernicus.eu/en/articles/4444611-citing-copernicus-marine-products-and-services

## EMODnet and GEBCO

The EMODnet bathymetry products are provided under the EMODnet terms of use; retain attribution and the applicable CC BY 4.0 requirements: https://emodnet.ec.europa.eu/en/terms-use-emodnet-online-services-data-and-data-products

GEBCO bathymetry is included for regional context. Follow the GEBCO terms and cite the specific grid release: https://www.gebco.net/data-products/gridded-bathymetry/terms-of-use

## GOT5.6 tide model

GOT5.6 constituent grids are distributed by NASA's Earth Sciences Division. Preserve and consult the accompanying `#README.Pub` file in `Data/Tide_Model/GOT5.6/`; cite the model and its source publication as specified there. Model predictions are not local tide-gauge observations.

## PANGAEA sediment data

The regional grain-size context file is PANGAEA dataset 207974. Its spatial distance from Anza is documented in the audit outputs; it is not treated as a local measured D50. Cite the dataset record and follow its stated license: https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.207974

## Code and other materials

No repository-wide software or data license is assigned here. Public visibility permits inspection of the materials; it does not create additional rights to redistribute third-party data or reuse the author-supplied survey. Follow the license and attribution terms attached to each source.
