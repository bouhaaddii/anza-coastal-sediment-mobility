Figure. Exploratory associations between Sentinel-2 NDTI and regional hydrodynamic context at acquisition dates. Panels show the median NDTI on the same 50,128 pixels observed in all 11 selected scenes against (A) maximum significant wave height during the preceding 24 h, (B) significant wave height at acquisition, (C) regional surface-current speed, and (D) the modeled GOT5.6 tide anomaly relative to the UTC-day mean. Point color and labels indicate acquisition date. Spearman coefficients are descriptive only; no p-values or causal interpretation are provided. The IBI fields are regional model context, the tide estimate has no local gauge validation, and NDTI is not calibrated to suspended-sediment concentration.
