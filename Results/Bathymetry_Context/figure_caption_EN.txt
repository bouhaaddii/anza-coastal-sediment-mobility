Figure. Local Anza soundings and open bathymetry products shown at their native map scales. Panel A displays the survey Z values relative to the local hydrographic zero of Port Agadir. Panels B and C show EMODnet and GEBCO regional grids, respectively; the orange points and dashed outline locate the local survey. The grids have substantially coarser spatial resolution and use vertical references different from the local survey; GEBCO cautions that some shallow-water source cells may use another vertical reference than nominal MSL. The map is a coverage and scale audit only; elevations have not been converted, compared statistically, resampled, or merged.
