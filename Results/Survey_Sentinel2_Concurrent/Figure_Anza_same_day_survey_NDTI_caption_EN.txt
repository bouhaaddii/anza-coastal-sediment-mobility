Figure. Same-day spatial comparison of the Anza bathymetric survey and Sentinel-2 NDTI on 10 November 2022. (A) Survey points whose containing 10 m pixel is clear and classified as water by SCL, colored by NDTI. (B) Median NDTI and local-zero depth aggregated to 100 m spatial blocks; the red line and error bars summarize medians and interquartile spatial dispersion across occupied 2 m depth bins. The block Spearman coefficient is a descriptive monotonic-rank summary and may not represent a non-monotonic depth response. (C) Fraction of survey points with a clear SCL-water pixel in each local-Z group; this is a coincidence diagnostic, not shoreline accuracy. The granule sensing time is compared with the reported 10:33–15:16 survey window assuming Africa/Casablanca local time; individual point times are unavailable. Z is not transformed from the Agadir port hydrographic zero, and NDTI is uncalibrated to suspended-sediment concentration.
