Figure. Daily valid-cell support and HR-OC SPM/TUR distributions over the Sentinel-2 stable-water core at Anza. A CMEMS cell is included when at least half its area overlaps the exploratory stable-water mask; 25% and 75% threshold sensitivities are retained in the accompanying table. The upper panel reports valid-water-cell coverage and counts, with selected Sentinel-2 acquisition dates highlighted. The lower panel shows daily SPM and TUR medians with spatial interquartile ranges. Date-specific valid footprints vary, and a same-day match does not establish use of the same MSI granule or constitute in-situ validation.
