Figure 1. Nested location and layered observation support for Anza, Morocco. (A) Three-dimensional view of the Sentinel-2 L2A RGB composite (bands B04/B03/B02; acquisition 10 November 2022) and the 22,986 submerged soundings from the local survey. Sounding depth is plotted positive downward from the hydrographic zero (ZH) of the Port of Agadir and encoded by colour. The RGB image plane is placed at an artificial depth of 26 m solely to separate the layers visually; it is not a bathymetric surface or a vertical-datum transformation. (B) Morocco locator with Anza/Agadir highlighted. (C) Local RGB view with the survey footprint outlined. The satellite and survey layers are co-located horizontally for context but were not vertically fused.
