Figure 2. Staged workflow for the Anza analysis. Inputs are grouped above their preprocessing and harmonisation steps. The primary analysis compares satellite acquisition-date groups with the full 182-day forcing record over a fixed bathymetric support. Secondary analyses evaluate satellite-product associations and repeated-cell wave response. All mobility results are model-conditional; the workflow does not represent field-calibrated suspended sediment concentration, sediment flux, transport direction, or a validated surf-zone simulation.
