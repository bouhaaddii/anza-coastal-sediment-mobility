Figure 2. Workflow of the Anza study. Sentinel-2 scenes, local soundings, regional wave/current forcing, modelled tide anomalies, and the satellite-derived HR-OC product are screened and harmonised for separate primary and secondary analyses. The primary analysis compares acquisition-date groups against the 182-day forcing reference on 40 fixed depth classes, using binary and continuous model-conditional mobility metrics. The secondary branch evaluates optical-product associations and their sensitivity to depth and spatial structure. Neither branch is an in-situ sediment-concentration or sediment-flux validation.
