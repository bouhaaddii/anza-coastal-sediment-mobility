Figure A. Bathymetry-conditioned scenario map of the modeled fraction of valid hourly records exceeding the incipient-motion threshold (M >= 1), for the 0.10 mm literature-informed but unmeasured near-bottom scenario and unadjusted local hydrographic zero. Aouiche et al. (2016) report this historical LCHF (1972) grain-size value for the bottom of Agadir Bay; it is not a measurement from the 2022 Anza survey. The same regional wave and current forcing is applied across the survey area; the spatial pattern mainly reflects depth bins, not resolved spatial variability in hydrodynamic forcing. No nearshore wave transformation is represented. This is not a sediment-flux map.

Figure B. Modeled fraction of valid hourly records with M >= 1 by local survey depth and assumed grain-size scenario for wave-only and combined wave-current stress, under two vertical-reference sensitivities. The 0.10 mm scenario is a historical near-bottom literature benchmark; 0.25 mm is a historical beach value; other values are analyst-defined sensitivity bounds. None is a measured D50 for the 2022 survey. The x-axis is displayed from 0.45 to resolve the high-exceedance range; valid-hour support varies with depth and is shown in Figure C. The +2.159 m case is an unverified candidate datum offset. Lines are conditional scenario frequencies, not confidence intervals, probabilities, or transport rates.

Figure C. Fraction of the 4,368-hour forcing record with valid wave-only or combined wave-current inputs by depth for the 0.10 mm literature-informed but unmeasured near-bottom scenario. Missing support is concentrated in shallow depth bins because of the breaker screen and the vertical range of the regional current profile.
