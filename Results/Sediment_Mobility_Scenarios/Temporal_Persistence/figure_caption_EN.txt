Figure. Month-by-depth frequency of modeled incipient-motion threshold exceedance (M ≥ 1) for the local hydrographic zero without datum adjustment. Panels compare the 0.10 mm literature-informed but unmeasured near-bottom scenario and the 1.00 mm coarse sensitivity under wave-only and combined wave plus daily regional-current forcing. Each monthly value is weighted by the number of valid hourly records in supported days (at least 12 valid hours per day); gray cells have fewer than 10 supported days. The 0.10 mm value is historical regional context reported for the bottom of Agadir Bay, not a measurement from the 2022 Anza survey. The map does not resolve nearshore wave transformation or spatial variation in regional forcing. Exceedance frequency is not a probability, a sediment-transport rate, or a field observation.
