Figure. Within-cell fixed-effect associations between daily Copernicus Marine HR-OC L3 SPM and mean IBI significant wave height summarized over nominal 0–24 h, 24–48 h, and 48–72 h windows referenced to a local Sentinel-2 acquisition time. Separate-window models estimate each wave window individually; joint distributed-lag models estimate conditional coefficients where estimable. Points show standardized coefficients and bars show exploratory two-axis block-bootstrap 95% intervals (500 m spatial blocks and moving blocks of three eligible acquisitions). The HR-OC L3 daily product aggregates Sentinel-2 tile mosaics and the source acquisition time per SPM pixel is unavailable in the downloaded subset; therefore these are nominal forcing-window associations, not verified physical response lags. HR-OC SPM was not validated against local in-situ SPM in this study, and no causal sediment-transport inference is made.
