Figure. Bathymetry-conditioned temporal response of the Copernicus Marine HR-OC SPM estimate to antecedent wave forcing at Anza. (A) Daily median SPM estimate over repeated cells containing local survey soundings, paired by calendar day with the maximum IBI significant wave height in the preceding 24 h. (B) Standardized within-cell fixed-effect coefficient for antecedent wave height, with exploratory intervals from resampling 500 m spatial blocks and moving blocks of three eligible acquisitions. The HR-OC source granule is not confirmed to be identical to the local Sentinel-2 product; modeled regional forcing and satellite-derived SPM are not in-situ validation.
