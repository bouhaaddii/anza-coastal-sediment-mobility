Figure S10. Temporal support of the Sentinel-2 acquisition catalog and the 11 scenes with image-based ROI quality assessment, shown against the saved daily model-conditional mobility score. Panel A shows the daily mean log10 of the 95th percentile of M across the 40 fixed depth bins used in the main analysis for the unadjusted local hydrographic zero and the unmeasured D50 = 0.25 mm scenario. Gray ticks mark unique catalog dates; orange diamonds identify the 11 ROI-QA scenes. Panel B gives monthly counts. The score is model output, not observed seabed mobility, suspended-sediment concentration, or flux.
