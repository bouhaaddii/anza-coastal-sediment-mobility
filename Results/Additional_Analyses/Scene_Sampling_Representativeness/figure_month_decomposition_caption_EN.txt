Figure. Decomposition of the processed 11-scene subset's modeled mobility-exceedance difference from the full 182-day reference. The gray component represents the effect of the subset's month composition, calculated by weighting each month's all-weather mean by the number of selected acquisition dates in that month. The orange component is the remaining within-month difference between selected scene dates and all-weather days in the same months. Points mark the total difference. Panels show the 0.10 mm literature-informed but unmeasured scenario and the 1.00 mm sensitivity under wave-only and wave plus daily regional-current forcing. This decomposition controls descriptively for month composition but does not establish a cloud-caused bias, observed SSC bias, or sediment flux.
