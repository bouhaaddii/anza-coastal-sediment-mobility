Figure. Differences in a continuous, model-based mobility-intensity indicator between Sentinel-2 sampling sets and the complete 182-day forcing record. The plotted score is the mean across fixed bathymetric depth classes of daily log10 of the 95th percentile of the modeled mobility ratio M. Blue circles show the unstandardized difference from the full-period mean; orange diamonds show the within-month difference after matching the full-period reference to the months represented in each image set. The six panels compare three unmeasured D50 scenarios and wave-only versus wave-plus-regional-daily-current forcing. Cloud cover is product-tile metadata. Results are descriptive and conditional on the regional forcing, local hydrographic zero, and Script 32 equations; they are not measurements of sediment flux, observed mobility, SSC, or causal effects.
