Descriptive spatial consistency diagnostic only. The Sentinel-2 SCL classes are derived from November 2022 to March 2023; the local bathymetric/topographic survey was acquired at a different time. Survey Z is relative to the local Agadir port hydrographic zero; no vertical transformation to instantaneous sea level, LAT, or MSL was applied. The projected survey coordinates use a candidate EPSG:26192 to UTM 29N transformation whose reported operation accuracy is 7 m. Therefore, class-versus-Z counts are not shoreline validation, datum validation, or a confusion-matrix accuracy assessment. Use the overlay to identify gross spatial inconsistencies and boundary-sensitive points only.
