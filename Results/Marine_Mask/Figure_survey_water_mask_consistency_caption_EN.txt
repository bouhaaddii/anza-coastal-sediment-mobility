Figure. Spatial and distributional comparison of the local Anza survey with the multi-scene Sentinel-2 SCL water-occurrence diagnostic. (A) Survey soundings are plotted over the fraction of clear observations classified as water; point colors show Z relative to the local Agadir port hydrographic zero. (B) Boxplots summarize survey Z at locations sampled from each SCL stability class; the dashed line marks the local zero. The image observations span November 2022–March 2023 and are not contemporaneous with the survey. This descriptive overlay is not a shoreline-accuracy or vertical-datum validation.
