Figure. Sentinel-2 SCL water-class occurrence across selected clear observations from November 2022 to March 2023. Panel A shows the proportion of clear observations classified as water. Panel B groups pixels into exploratory stability classes using a minimum-observation requirement and stated occurrence thresholds; the dashed outline shows the local bathymetric survey extent. The variable fringe may include shoreline migration, intertidal water, surf, mixed pixels, and classification error. This is not a surveyed coastline and must not be used as a final hydrodynamic boundary without comparison to a suitable reference shoreline.
